# Monthly Stock Market Master Research Script – India

**1. Gather Macro and Technical Market Context**
- Capture latest Nifty and Sensex levels, technical patterns, year-end analyst targets.
- Track FII/DII flows and currency moves for the month.
- Note major RBI policy moves and India/US/EU/China macro events.

**2. Identify Sectoral Tailwinds and Headwinds**
- Review recent sectoral indices: Nifty IT, Nifty Pharma, Nifty Bank, Nifty PSU Bank, Nifty Metal, Nifty FMCG, Nifty Consumer Durables.
- Note the top 3 rising and falling sectors last month.
- Note major earnings, regulatory, or policy actions in each sector.

**3. Screen for Strong Fundamental Stocks**
- Set screener parameters:
    - Market cap > ₹10,000 crore (or top 70%)
    - PE Ratio between 10 and 30
    - ROE above 15%
    - Debt to Equity below 1
    - Revenue Growth > 10% QoQ/YoY
- Focus searches on: IT, Pharma, Banking, and one or two emerging sectors (e.g., Renewable Energy, Consumer Electronics).

**4. Rank by Composite Algorithm**
- For every stock meeting criteria, calculate:
    - Composite Score = (0.3 × ROE) + (0.25 × Revenue Growth) + (0.25 × Net Income Growth) + (0.2 × 1/PE)
    - Rank the top 10 overall, and top-performers per sector.
- Check for outliers and validate news/events/earnings for these stocks.

**5. Prepare Investment Shortlist**
- Tier 1: Highest Conviction Picks (composite + qualitative signals)
- Tier 2: Strong Fundamental Plays (less momentum or more risk)
- Large Cap Stalwarts (for capital preservation/defensive allocation)

**6. Strategy Construction**
- Aggressive: 40% Small/Mid Cap Growth, 35% Leaders, 25% Blue-Chips
- Balanced: 60% Large Cap, 30% Quality Mid-Cap, 10% Small Cap
- Validate allocation against risk tolerance & sector weights.

**7. Calendar of Catalysts**
- Map upcoming results (earnings), policy meetings, global data, and India festival season dynamics.
- Note any known trigger events for your shortlist names.

**8. Risk & Stop Loss Management**
- For each stock: Set max drawdown (e.g., 12–15%), planned stop loss, and trailing stop as price moved up.
- List key macro/market risk watchpoints for the month.

**9. Review/Backtest and Execute**
- Compare this month’s top picks with last month: What worked, what failed, and why?
- Document new portfolio buys, sells, and rebalances.
- Place trades as per model and update all data/notes for next review cycle.
