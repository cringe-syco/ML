### Stock Screening Strategy: “Earnings Growth + Re-rating”

1. **Earnings Growth:**  
     - EPS or Net Profit Growth ≥ 20%  
     - Sales Growth > 10%  
     - Operating Margin stable/increasing

2. **Low to Mid P/E:**  
     - P/E < 15  
     - P/E below industry average or 3-yr median

3. **Supporting Filters:**  
     - ROCE > 12%  
     - Debt-to-Equity < 1.5  
     - Promoter Holding > 50%  
     - Positive Cash Flow

**Example Screener Query (Screener.in):**
```text
Net Profit latest quarter > 20% AND 
Quarterly Sales Growth > 10% AND 
PE < 15 AND 
Return on capital employed > 12 AND 
Debt to equity < 1.5
```

- Compare current P/E vs 3-year average
- Look for sector tailwinds and sentiment change
- Tag stocks for watchlist, track next 2 quarters
